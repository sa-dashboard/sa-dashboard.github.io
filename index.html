Social Analytics (personal)

A personal, self-hosted analytics tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It reads performance statistics for the owner's own social media accounts (such as YouTube) and shows them in dashboards organized by project.

The application is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own accounts.

What it does

  • Lists the videos published on the connected YouTube channel.
  • Reads view, like, comment and watch-time statistics for those videos and for the channel.
  • Stores these statistics locally to show trends over time.

Access is read-only: the application never uploads, edits or deletes anything on YouTube.
